❓ How to Sail

🌬️ The wind arrow shows where the wind is blowing. Sails catch the wind and push you along!

🚫 The No-Go Zone: You can NEVER sail straight into the wind — your sail just flaps and you stop! The red cone shows the no-go zone. To go upwind, zigzag: sail at an angle, then TACK (turn your bow through the wind) and sail at an angle the other way.

⛵ Trim your sail: Pull it in (W) when sailing toward the wind, let it out (S) when sailing away from the wind. If the sail flaps — pull in! If you feel slow and tipped over — let out!

🔁 Tacking: turning your front through the wind. Do it with speed so you don't get stuck "in irons"!

🔄 Gybing: turning your back through the wind. Watch out — the boom swings across! Pull the sail in a bit first for a smooth gybe.

Controls: A/D or ◀/▶ steer · W/▲ sail in · S/▼ sail out · Q/E jib (big boats) · SPACE water gun · V switch boat-view / bird-view · N no-go cone on/off · M sound
