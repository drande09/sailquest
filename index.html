❓ How to Sail

🌬️ The wind arrow shows where the wind is blowing. Sails catch the wind and push you along!

🚫 The No-Go Zone: You can NEVER sail straight into the wind — your sail just flaps and you stop! The red cone shows the no-go zone. To go upwind, zigzag: sail at an angle, then TACK (turn your bow through the wind) and sail at an angle the other way.

⛵ Trim your sail: Pull it in (W) as you head toward the wind, and let it out (S) as you turn away. If it flaps outside the no-go zone, pull in until it fills. Inside the no-go zone, steer away from the wind first.

🔁 Tacking: turning your front through the wind. Do it with speed so you don't get stuck "in irons"!

🔄 Gybing: turning your stern through the wind. Bring the mainsail toward the middle to control the boom, turn smoothly, then ease the sail on the new side.

Points of sail: A beam reach puts the wind at your side (90°). A close reach heads more toward the wind; a broad reach heads farther away. Running puts the wind behind you. The wind dial always puts the wind source at the top and turns the little boat with your heading.

Views: Choose 3D chase to watch the whole boat, First person to sit at the helm, or Overhead to see your upwind zigzags. Drag the water to look around, and use Look ahead to recenter.

Controls: A/D or left/right arrows steer the boat left/right · W sail in · S sail out · Q/E jib · SPACE water gun · V view · N learning guides · M sound.

A simplified sailing trainer: wind and trim are made forgiving so you can practice. A real tiller moves opposite the bow's turn. Practice on the water with an instructor; boat handling varies by boat and conditions.
